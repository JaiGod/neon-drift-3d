HOW TO PLAY

  • Drift between 3 lanes: ← → or A D. On phones, swipe or tap the left / right side.
  • Dodge traffic and drones. One hit and the run is over.
  • Pass cars in the next lane for a NEAR MISS. Chain them to raise your multiplier (up to x5).
  • Cyan boost pads give a burst of speed. While boosting you smash through traffic.
  • Collect coins to unlock colorways in the Garage.
  • The Daily Run uses the same track for everyone each day.
  • P/Esc pause · M sound
